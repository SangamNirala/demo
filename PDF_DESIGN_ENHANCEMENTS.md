## 🎯 Design Principles Applied

### 1. **Consistency**
- Uniform spacing throughout (0.1-0.25 inch)
- Consistent border widths (1-3pt)
- Standardized padding (10-15pt)

### 2. **Hierarchy**
- Clear visual distinction between sections
- Size and color indicate importance
- Icons provide quick visual cues

### 3. **Readability**
- Generous white space
- Proper line height (14pt leading)
- High contrast text
- Justified body text

### 4. **Color Psychology**
- Red: Urgent, critical
- Orange: Important, attention needed
- Blue: Information, trust
- Green: Positive, success
- Purple: Planning, strategy

### 5. **Professional Appearance**
- Clean borders and boxes
- Subtle backgrounds
- No clutter
- Balanced layout

---

## 📏 Layout Specifications

### Page Setup
- **Size:** Letter (8.5" × 11")
- **Margins:** 
  - Top: 0.5"
  - Bottom: 0.6"
  - Left/Right: 0.75"
- **Content Width:** 7" (6.5" for most elements)

### Spacing
- **Section Spacing:** 0.15-0.25"
- **Element Spacing:** 0.08-0.12"
- **Paragraph Spacing:** 6pt after

### Borders
- **Section Headers:** 2pt solid
- **Content Boxes:** 1-2pt solid
- **Tables:** 0.5-1pt grid, 2pt outer

### Padding
- **Headers:** 10-12pt
- **Content Boxes:** 12-15pt
- **Tables:** 10pt
